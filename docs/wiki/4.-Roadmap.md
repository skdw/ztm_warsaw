# 🗺️ Roadmap

There is no list of promises here, because a copy would go stale. Where things are written down:

- **What changed and what is coming in the next version:** the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/solarssk/ztm_warsaw/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), under *Unreleased*.
- **What is planned and what is broken:** the [issues](https://github.com/solarssk/ztm_warsaw/issues) and the milestones.
- **How a release is made:** the header of the [release workflow](https://github.com/solarssk/ztm_warsaw/blob/main/.github/workflows/release.yml).

The integration depends on the City of Warsaw's public API, which publishes no schedule for its own changes.
